Throughout this web development course, I have learned many new skills and gained a much better understanding of how websites are designed and built. When I first started the course, I felt excited but also nervous because I had very little experience with web design and programming. I was unsure if I would be able to learn HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I worried that coding would be too difficult for me. However, I was interested in learning how websites work and hoped to create my own website by the end of the course.

One of the biggest challenges I faced was understanding how HTML, CSS, and JavaScript work together. HTML was easier to learn because it focuses on the structure of a webpage, but CSS and JavaScript were more challenging. I sometimes struggled with styling elements correctly or finding errors in my code. To overcome these challenges, I practiced regularly, reviewed examples, and experimented with different solutions. I also used online resources, classroom materials, and feedback from my teacher and classmates to improve my understanding.

The design thinking process played an important role in my learning. It taught me to focus on the needs of users and clients rather than only on the appearance of a website. During the development of my portfolio website, I created design plans, gathered feedback from users and clients, and made improvements based on their suggestions. For example, I improved the layout, adjusted colors, added images, and made navigation easier to use. This process helped me understand the importance of testing and iteration in web design.

Looking back, I have become much more confident in my abilities. I can now create multi-page websites using HTML, style them with CSS, and add interactive features using JavaScript. I also have a better understanding of user experience, design principles, and project planning. Overall, this course has helped me develop both technical and problem-solving skills, and I am proud of the progress I have made throughout the term.
